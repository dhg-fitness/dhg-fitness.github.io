DHG Fitness - Top 30 Protein + Fibre Foods

A sortable guide for people who want more filling meals, improved health and easier weight-loss choices.Figures are shown per typical portion, not per 100g.

Click any column heading to sort. Click again to reverse the order. Default view is lowest calories per portion first.

Notes: Values are approximate and vary by brand, cooking method and drained weight. Foods are plain single-ingredient versions. Beans, lentils, chickpeas and peas are shown cooked or drained as eaten. Oats, brans, seeds, nuts and TVP are shown as dry weight. Suggested daily portions are a practical guide for most healthy adults, not a personalised nutrition plan.
